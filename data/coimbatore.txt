Coimbatore Travel Guide

Coimbatore is a major city in Tamil Nadu.

Marudamalai Temple is located on a hill and is a
popular religious destination.

The Isha Yoga Center is located near the
Velliangiri foothills and is known for the
Adiyogi statue.

VOC Park is a recreational destination.

Gedee Car Museum is a museum featuring
automobile collections.

Coimbatore has many restaurants serving
South Indian cuisine.
